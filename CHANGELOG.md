# Catatan rilis

## Berikutnya

- **Tema terang** dan **8 warna aksen** (Pengaturan › Umum). Bisa juga ikut mode terang/gelap Windows.
- **Galeri widget pakai preview:** tiap widget keliatan bentuk aslinya sebelum ditambah.
- **Pengaturan bisa dicari** (`Ctrl+F`), plus bagian **Lanjutan** buat setelan yang jarang disentuh dan tombol buat balikin notch ke tengah.
- Notch melar kenyal kayak pegas, angka di semua widget pakai skala yang sama, widget Minum didesain ulang.

## 0.4.0

**Baru**

- **Tour perkenalan.** Pas pertama buka, Pip ngenalin fitur yang nggak keliatan: geser notch, atur widget, Rak, Pip desktop, cara main, dan fitur Claude yang opsional. Bisa diulang dari Pengaturan › Umum.
- **Bahasa Inggris.** Semua teks (notch, widget, pengaturan, omongan Pip, menu tray, pesan error) ada versi Inggris-nya. Otomatis ngikutin bahasa Windows, atau pilih sendiri di Pengaturan › Umum.
- **Update otomatis.** PoinTify ngecek GitHub Releases tiap nyala dan nawarin pasang + restart. Bisa dimatiin, dan bisa dicek manual di Pengaturan › Tentang.
- **Kanvas widget di Beranda** dengan 22 widget, mode Atur, dan Live di notch kecil.
- **Pip desktop jadi pet beneran:** fisika, nangkring di jendela, dielus, kejar-kejaran, main sama kursor, dikasih makan file, ikut ngetik, dan ikut nonton pakai kacamata 3D + popcorn.
- Notch bisa digeser ke kiri-kanan (Pip-nya ikut kegoncang), lirik jalan di tengah notch kecil, ikon asli di widget Peluncur, cuaca dan jadwal sholat ngikutin lokasi PC, ikon file per ekstensi di Rak, timer fokus yang didesain ulang.

**Diperbaiki**

- Pengaturan nggak "balik lagi" kalau diubah barengan sama posisi Pip, dan file pengaturan nggak bisa rusak pas PC mati mendadak.
- Satu widget error nggak lagi bikin notch kosong.
- Clipboard cuma dipantau kalau widget Clipboard dipasang.
- Kota bawaan cuaca dan sholat ngikutin zona waktu PC (sebelumnya selalu Nabire).
- Prompt bawaan di widget Prompt cepat sekarang beneran nyisipin teks yang lagi dicopy.
- Sesi feed nggak nyangkut di "lagi kerja", dan Pip lebih ringan di CPU.

# Release notes

## Next

- **Light theme** and **8 accent colors** (Settings › General). It can also follow Windows' light/dark mode.
- **Widget gallery with previews:** every widget shows what it actually looks like before you add it.
- **Searchable settings** (`Ctrl+F`), plus an **Advanced** section for rarely used options and a button to re-center the notch.
- The notch stretches open with a springy bounce, numbers across all widgets share one scale, and the Water widget was redesigned.

## 0.4.0

**New**

- **Welcome tour.** On first launch, Pip walks you through the hidden features: moving the notch, arranging widgets, the Shelf, desktop Pip, how to play, and the optional Claude features. Replay it from Settings › General.
- **English.** Every piece of text (notch, widgets, settings, Pip's lines, tray menu, error messages) is available in English. It follows your Windows language automatically, or pick one in Settings › General.
- **Automatic updates.** PoinTify checks GitHub Releases on startup and offers to install and restart. You can turn it off, or check manually in Settings › About.
- **Widget canvas on Home** with 22 widgets, Arrange mode, and Live in the small notch.
- **Desktop Pip is a real pet now:** physics, perching on windows, petting, chase, cursor play, feeding him files, typing along, and watching videos with 3D glasses and popcorn.
- The notch can be dragged left and right (Pip gets shaken along), lyrics scroll in the small notch, real app icons in the Launcher, weather and prayer times follow your PC's location, per-extension file icons on the Shelf, and a redesigned focus timer.

**Fixed**

- Settings no longer revert when changed at the same time as Pip's position, and the settings file can't get corrupted by a sudden shutdown.
- A single broken widget no longer blanks the whole notch.
- The clipboard is only monitored while the Clipboard widget is on Home.
- The default weather and prayer city follows your PC's time zone (it used to always be Nabire).
- The default quick prompts now actually insert the copied text.
- Feed sessions no longer get stuck on "working", and Pip uses less CPU.
